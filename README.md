# 📊 Finance Performance Dashboard (Excel)
**Tools:** Excel • Pivot Tables • Slicers • KPI Cards • Pie/Bar/Line Charts  
**Period Covered:** FY 2013–2014  
**Date Created:** 16 Nov 2025  

## 📘 Overview  
This project analyzes a  dataset for FY 2013–2014 and builds an interactive Excel dashboard to highlight:

- Revenue trends (Monthly + Quarterly)
- Net Profit trends
- Operating Expenses by Segment
- Country-wise Net Sales
- Product-wise Revenue + Profitability
- COGS vs Net Profit comparison
- Discount impact on margins

The dashboard supports dynamic interactivity using **Slicers** for:  
`Segment`, `Country`, `Product`.

---

## 🎯 Decision Context
This Excel dashboard is designed to support high-level profitability review and cost diagnostics, enabling management to identify margin pressure, expense-heavy segments, and regions requiring deeper investigation.

---

## 🚫 Scope & Limitations
This analysis is based on historical financial data and descriptive metrics.  
It does not perform forecasting, scenario simulation, or prescriptive strategy recommendations.

## 📊 Dashboard Insights Summary

### 🟧 Segment Analysis
- Government & Small Business have highest operating expenses.  
- Enterprise drives high revenue with moderate expenses.

### 🟫 Discount Impact
- High & Medium discounts → best profit margins.  
- No-discount group shows lowest margins (~31%).  
- Discounts appear to support higher-volume profitability within certain discount bands.

### 🔵 Revenue & Profit Trends
- Q4 is the strongest quarter in both 2013 & 2014.  
- Highest yearly net profit in Oct–Dec 2013 & 2014.  
- Net sales trend rises strongly toward late 2014.

### 🟠 Market Insights (Country)
- USA & Canada lead revenue share.  
- Germany, France, Mexico ~18–21% each.

### 🟩 Product Performance
- Paseo leads in total revenue.  
- Amarilla shows strongest profit efficiency.  
- Velo & VTT mid-performers.

### 🟣 Cost & Efficiency
- Higher COGS % = lower profit.  
- Lower COGS products (like Amarilla) show highest profitability.
